Beyond the Map

A journey through the pulse of Greek rap.

The Vibe

LyricMap isn't just about data and coordinates—it's about the energy that fills the air when the bass drops. Since 2016, I've been front and center at rap concerts, witnessing the evolution of the scene firsthand.

There's something raw about a live show that a studio recording can't capture. It's the sweat, the adrenaline, and the shared connection between the artist and the crowd. From small underground spots to massive arenas, I've been there, soaking it all in.

The Lens

Parallel to the music, I developed a deep passion for concert photography. Capturing that one perfect moment—the artist in full flow, the raw emotion of the crowd—is what keeps me going. It's about freezing a split second of history.

This map is my way of connecting those dots—the lyrics we scream along to, the places they mention, and the memories forged in the pit.
