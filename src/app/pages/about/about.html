BEYOND_THE_MAP

A visual archive of the Greek underground.

THE_VIBE

LyricMap isn't just data points on a screen. it's the raw energy of the pit. since 2016, i've been documenting the evolution of greek rap from the front row.

there's something honest about a live show. the adrenaline, the sweat, the connection. it's about the moments that don't make it to the record.

THE_LENS

photography became my way of freezing those moments. capturing the grit, the flow, and the raw emotion of the scene. every shot is a piece of history.

this map connects the tracks we scream to the cities that birthed them. it's a tribute to the culture.
