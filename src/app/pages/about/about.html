BEYOND_THE_MAP

A visual archive of the Greek underground.

THE_VIBE

LyricMap isn't just data points on a screen. It's a representation of the locations important to the culture. It pinpoints the hubs from which the most important sounds have emerged.

The culture is diverse, spanning multiple genres and styles, and LyricMap aims to capture it all. It gives the listener a new way to engage with the music, connecting the lyrics to the places where they were born.

THE_LENS

Photography became my way of freezing those moments. capturing the energy, the emotion, and the vibe of the culture. Every shot is a piece of history.

This map connects the tracks we scream to the places that birthed them. It's a tribute to the culture.
